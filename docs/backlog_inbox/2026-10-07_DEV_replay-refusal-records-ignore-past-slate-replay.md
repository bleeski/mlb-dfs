# 2026-10-07 DEV (Session 155, R488-R490): a `--past-slate-replay` refusal still writes a delivery record into `data/deliveries/`

**Observed (read in the tree at 7c0637e while planning the 1600_4g replay).** R283 made a replay "never RECORDED and never PROMOTED" (`build_slate.py` ~:9338-9351: `_REPLAY_BARRED` reaches `run_slate(replay=...)` and the Showdown writers). The refusal recorder is the other writer and does not read it: `_main_recording_refusals` (`build_slate.py` ~:9583-9641) calls `delivery_record.write_refusal_record(...)` on every non-zero exit with no `_REPLAY_BARRED` check, so a replay that refuses (exit 3, 4 or 10) lands a `refusal` record under `<artifact root>/data/deliveries/<date>/` beside the live slate's real records. The 1600_4g replays in Session 155 ran with `MLB_DFS_ARTIFACT_ROOT` pointed at a scratch directory for exactly this reason (the memory note "a scratch run_slate writes real records into data/deliveries/ and the classifier blocks deleting them").

**Why it matters.** A post-slate replay of a refused slate (the normal way to verify a fix, as Session 155 did) writes refusal records that read as the live slate's own history to `awaiting_standings`, retros and any reader of `data/deliveries/`. The R283 sentence "a replay is never RECORDED" is false for the refusal half.

**Candidate item.** In `_main_recording_refusals`, skip `write_refusal_record` when `_REPLAY_BARRED` (print that the replay's refusal was not recorded), or stamp `replay: true` on the record and teach the readers to skip it. A test: a `--past-slate-replay` build that refuses at exit 4 writes nothing under `data/deliveries/`. Effort XS. Nothing here is a lift, an edge or a win rate.
