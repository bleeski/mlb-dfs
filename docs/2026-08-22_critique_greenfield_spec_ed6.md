## 1.2 First-principles findings

**What the system is.** A deterministic, evidence-bound lineup factory: DK files in by hand, certified CSV out by hand, everything between is code with gates that block on None. The LLM sits at the edges (unstructured intake, orchestration, review) and is contractually out of the math. That boundary is correct and already matches what every prior edition's LLM-vs-deterministic table recommends.

**Where the reviews keep converging.** Five editions plus this one all land on the same three structural truths. (1) The objective is honest: proxies labeled as proxies, no EV claim without a calibrated model, and the sim/economics ladder gated behind R13/R10 plus the variance-basis and scenario-bank preconditions. (2) The certification chain (immutable runs, re-read-the-exported-file gates, preflight as sole pre-upload rule) is the right skeleton, and its residual defects are point fixes (this edition's R172–R178), not design flaws. (3) The strategic bottleneck is measurement, not generation — the engine can produce legal, diverse, controlled portfolios faster than the loop can grade them.

**The defect texture worth naming.** Of 32 filed items, 24 are silence defects: a thing that degrades, mislabels, or crashes WITHOUT telling the operator (coverage that skips a fetch after a scratch; a manifest field that says "enriched" on proxy builds; a gate certifying on a stub; a supervisor that loses its own decision log; an eval red blamed on the wrong cause). The project's own F4 doctrine ("a gate is True only when something checkable says so") is the right immune system, and most of these are places the doctrine has not yet reached — especially code landed in the last 96 hours (R143/R151 surfaces account for 5 items). New code is where the defects live; the old core held up under adversarial review.

**Waste, measured not asserted.** The optimizer reviewer measured a production-shaped 120-row pool: single solve 0.072s total (54% inside HiGHS); by n=10 multi-lineup, 11.38s with 97% inside HiGHS — accumulated overlap rows harden branch-and-bound, and the R87 matrix-rebuild lever is worth ~3% on this shape. Conclusion recorded on R87: `mip_rel_gap` on bank solves is the only lever touching the mass, and no measurement argues for a solver migration. The bank job grid wastes solves two ways (R164: measured 15 identical solves for 3 candidates under heavy pins; ~528 provably infeasible jobs on a 12-game slate), and the DU ladder re-solves an identical problem 51 times where 11 suffice (R163). All patch-sized.

## 1.5 Zero-touch boundary

Zero-touch is defined as: everything between (click 1) Ben downloading DKSalaries/DKEntries and dropping them into the project, and (click 2) Ben uploading the certified CSV at DraftKings. Inside that span the target is no prompt adjustments and no mid-run debugging — which is what autobuild (R134) plus the counted-relaxation policy already implement, and what the lost-window items filed here (R167, R168, R169, R132, R124, R115) are exactly the remaining distance to. Outside that span, automation is refused by standing wall: DK prohibits scripted access, the project's rule is stricter than DK's, and this edition (like ed5's compliance section) treats the two clicks as design constraints, not friction to engineer away. ## 4.2 Academic and quant literature

**Hunter, Vielma & Zaman, "Picking Winners" (arXiv 1604.01455, 2016).** Portfolio objective: max P(at least one of k entries clears a high threshold t) under jointly Gaussian lineup scores; pairwise bound U₂(S) = Σ P(E_i) − ½ Σ P(E_i ∩ E_j); submodularity gives greedy sequential construction ≥ 1−1/e ≈ 63.2% of optimal. Per-lineup MILP: max expected points s.t. $50k cap, roster/positions, team diversity, variance-increasing stacking (five CONSECUTIVE batters allowing cyclic orders like 8-9-1-2-3; DK's 5-hitter cap), never pair a pitcher with opposing hitters (negative correlation), and an overlap cap vs all prior lineups (γ=6 of 10 for baseball). Empirics: 10 DK MLB contests 2016 — one outright 1st of 47,916. Reference implementation: github.com/zlisto/Daily-Fantasy-Baseball-Contests-in-DraftKings.

**Haugh & Singal, "How to Play Fantasy Sports Strategically (and Win)" (Management Science 67(1), 2021).** Opponent model: positional ownership Dirichlet(α), Dirichlet REGRESSION α=exp(Xβ) on expected points, cost, public ownership estimates, home/away, fit Bayesian in STAN; opponent lineups sampled multinomially with a copula mixture C = (1−q)C_ind + qC_stack for strategic stacking (q per contest type). Objectives: double-up maximizes P(wᵀδ > G^(r′)) against the cash-line ORDER STATISTIC of opponent scores; top-heavy with N entries sums payout-curve increments times P(beating rank-indexed order statistics), i.e., the payout ladder and tie handling enter through payout-at-rank. Solved as mean-variance binary quadratic programs over a λ grid (max wᵀμ + λ(wᵀΣw − 2wᵀσ_{δ,G})); replication-vs-diversification theorem (replicate the best lineup iff μ¹₁/μ¹₂ > (O₁+2)/O₁); overlap cap γ = C−3 = 6. Real-money 2017 NFL: $280.74 profit on $75.76 staked (top-heavy strategic) vs $91.50 projection-only; rake ≈15% noted. THE canonical cash-vs-GPP split: cash minimizes variance vs the cash-line order statistic; top-heavy maximizes λ-weighted variance and NEGATIVE covariance with the field.

**Successors/adjacent:** Newell & Easton 2017 (scenario-based stochastic IP; paywalled), Mlčoch et al. 2024 (generative opponent models; paywalled), Papageorgiou et al. 2024 (per-player ML ensembles MAPE ~29% + LP, NBA, top 18.4% of 11,764 users), Grody et al. 2024 (LP baseline persists). Bankroll: no canonical DFS-Kelly paper; industry flat-fraction norms (≤10%/day, 80/20 cash/GPP); fractional-Kelly theory transfers; treat Kelly inputs as unavailable absent a calibrated contest-EV distribution and stay flat-fraction.

## 4.3 MLB-specific quantitative structure

DK Classic frame (third-party, Aug 2026): 10 slots (2P/C/1B/2B/3B/SS/3OF), $50k, max 5 hitters/team; scoring 1B=3, 2B=5, 3B=8, HR=10, R=2, RBI=2, BB/HBP=2, SB=5; pitching 2.25/IP, 2/K, W=4, ER=−2, hits/BB against −0.6. Hitter-hitter correlation by order distance: no current public numeric matrix (vendors proprietary); best public evidence (Anderson, 13,449 games): strongest 1-2, then 1-3/4-5/2-3; when a hitter clears 10 DK points the #2 hitter gains +1.79 over average; 94% chance two-plus teammates co-occur in the day's top ten; later work finds 3-4 the single best pair on first-inning dynamics. Structural mechanism (Hunter et al.): runs/RBI double-count across consecutive batters; cyclic adjacency counts. Hitter-vs-opposing-pitcher: direction negative and universal; published magnitude not found — enforce the constraint, do not quote a coefficient. Batting order → opportunity: PA/game slot 1 = 4.48, slot 4 = 4.20, slot 9 = 3.47 (2022-23); DK points per total base flat across slots — order is opportunity, not efficiency. Distributions: SP DK percentiles 25th=5.8, median=13.3, 75th=21.2; hitters right-skewed with fat zero mass (Trout: zero hits in 30% of 2020 games); open-source encodes gamma hitters / Gaussian pitchers. Per-position/salary-tier CV tables: NOT public — a gap the archive can fill (feeds the sim-gate variance-basis precondition). Stack shapes: public numbers are vendor claims ("72% of GPP winners 4+ stacks", "5-3 most common winning structure"); the 5-hitter cap makes 5-3 the max-correlation legal shape and Hunter et al. the formal argument. Showdown: CPT 1.5x points/salary; leverage math is duplication-driven — ETR (Aug 2025, NFL showdown): product-of-ownership predicts dupes (r²=0.43, best single variable), completing the chalk stack raises expected dupes 5.1 → 10.1, underdog captains in 9+ spreads 39.9% less duplicated. Park/weather sizing: FanGraphs park factors regressed and halved for composite use; Kagan/THT: 10mph tailwind ≈ +29ft on a 100mph/34° fly; Wrigley wind-out HR rate >23% of flies vs 13% blowing in. Vegas → runs: implied team total = total split by spread; RotoGrinders calibration (1,196 games): actuals +0.33 vs implied, mean abs miss ≈2.4 runs; FantasyLabs Plus/Minus buckets: hitters +EV above ~4.0 implied, 4.6-6.0 strongest (top-6 order +1.43 vs −0.23 for 7-9); modern screens 5.0+/5.5+. Per-PA simulation architecture: the academic standard is a Markov chain on 24 base-out states + absorbing state (Bukiet-Harold-Palacios 1997; Analyzing Baseball Data with R ch.9), player-parameterized — commercial "play-by-play sims" are claimed versions of exactly this, and the chain is usable ANALYTICALLY (expected PA by slot, run distributions) without Monte Carlo.

## 4.4 Ownership and duplication modeling

Feature sets: industry qualitative (salary/value, projections consensus, batting order, Vegas, matchup perception, scarcity, recency/star bias); the only peer-reviewed fully specified model is Haugh-Singal's Dirichlet regression, which doubles as a generative field sampler with a stacking copula. Calibration metrics: NO vendor publishes ownership MAE-by-bucket or rank-correlation accuracy — the honest scoreboard is bucketed MAE + Spearman on archived actuals, which the owner can compute and vendors cannot show. Duplication empirics: 2015-16 Milly Makers — most-duplicated lineup ~121 copies; ~30% of full-$50k lineups were duplicates, dupes falling sharply with salary left; ETR: Πownership r²=0.43; SaberSim ships an expected-dupes number as a claim. Workable deterministic proxy consistent with all three: E[dupes] ≈ F · Π(own_i) · κ(salary_left, template), κ fit on archived contest data; label it a duplication-risk index, never a count, until calibrated.

## 4.5 Contest economics

Rake (DK NFL, Aug 2025, structurally transferable): $5 3-max 15.9%, $10 10-max 15.1%, $15 SE 15%, $50 11.97%, SE double-ups ~13%; historical anchor ~15.9% GPP margin; 2026 MLB-specific rake-by-tier: not findable without touching DK — the archived contest files are the right primary source. Payout curves: large GPPs reserve ~25-40% of the pool for the top 1%, first typically ≤10% of pool; cash 2x pays ~44% of field; selection heuristics prefer flatter tops and smaller fields. Satellites: all tickets equal-value → payout is a step function at the qualification rank; poker satellite math transfers (marginal score above the bubble worth zero); one-ticket satellite = winner-take-all → Hunter et al.'s max-P(win) applies literally; many-ticket qualifiers push back toward cash-style construction as tickets/field grows. Max-entry effects: Haugh-Singal's replication threshold + overlap-capped greedy portfolios formalize why 150-max regulars submit families of correlated-but-distinct stacks while single-entry collapses toward one balanced build; SaberSim's per-stakes field models are the commercial acknowledgment that entry limits change opponent behavior.

## 4.6 What the benchmark implies for a solo deterministic engine

Minimum competitive feature set by evidence strength: (1) correlation-aware stacking constraints (5-consecutive-cyclic primary + secondary; never pitcher-vs-opposing-hitters; the 5-hitter cap) — peer-reviewed + replicated, and ALREADY SHIPPED here; (2) portfolio diversification via overlap caps with greedy/joint construction (γ≈6) — proven approximation guarantee, SHIPPED (max_shared_players); (3) Vegas/park/order screens — well-calibrated public data, SHIPPED (F1/F2/F5); (4) ownership/duplication leverage at portfolio level — strong empirics, weak public calibration, SEQUENCED (R10/R118); (5) contest-shape-conditioned construction — solid theory, SHIPPED (postures/archetypes) with the satellite cell as the measured priority; (6) late-swap re-solve with locked constraints — SHIPPED; EV sizing unpublished anywhere.

(ii) A deterministic proxy layer can honestly do: everything in 1-6, plus a duplication-risk INDEX ∝ F·Πown·κ(salary_left), a leverage score (projection rank minus ownership rank), satellite/cash/GPP objective switches, lock-constrained re-solves — all truthfully labeled. The machinery that actually won contests in the literature is deterministic given projections. (iii) The proprietary edge: the archived own-contest standings contain realized ownership by slate, realized duplication by construction, realized payout/rake by contest, and own percentiles — the exact ground truth to calibrate an ownership model with metrics no vendor publishes, fit the dupes index, and build MLB rake/payout tables unobtainable without touching DK.
