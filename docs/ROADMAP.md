## How this file works

- **Authority.**
  - This file alone orders the work and records its status.
  - `docs/backlog.md` is the **R-entry register**: the What/Why/Fix body of each R-number named here. Read an entry with `grep -n "^### R<num>\." docs/backlog.md`; never read the file whole.
  - `CHANGELOG.md` records what shipped and why.
  - Nothing else is a backlog. A new item is filed as a register entry AND a row (or row edit) here, in the same commit. Fragments still arrive in `docs/backlog_inbox/`, and only DEV merges them.
- **The linter.** `python tools/plan_status.py --check` fails the gate when:
  - an open register entry (a `### R` heading without `CLOSED`) is named nowhere here;
  - a non-Deferred row names no R-number;
  - a Status is outside the vocabulary;
  - **NEXT** is not a Pending or In Progress row.

  `--print` gives a one-line-per-session view.
- **Status vocabulary:** `Pending`, `In Progress`, `Complete <YYYY-MM-DD>`, `Deferred`.
  - A batched block is Complete only when every item in it is.
  - A partial landing rewrites the row to its remainder, and the ledger records what landed.
- **Packaging.**
  - `Standalone`: one work unit with high blast radius (solver, state machine, certification, orchestration).
  - `Batched`: 2-4 cohesive, lower-risk units on one subsystem. One commit, one CHANGELOG entry per R-number.
- **Sizing.**
  - Every block fits one Claude Code context window without compaction. `docs/hosts.md` has the gate's time on each host.
  - Each row names its breakpoint, the point where a partial landing is still a valid commit.
  - A block that outgrows its window lands the finished part and splits its row.
- **IDs.** `Session NN` is the only live ID, two or three digits (R413: two-digit IDs run out at 99, and 89 is the last free one). `was CC-N` and `old Session N` in Source Origin resolve references written before 2026-09-22; those tables sit verbatim under `# Board history` in the register. 88 is the ARCHIVE block; 90-92 are the Deferred pools.

## Operating principles

1. **Delivery first** (Ben, 2026-09-22). A legally valid, accessible portfolio before the effective deadline outranks optional research, weather and odds lookups, simulation and quality gates. Since Sessions 10-12 every Classic and Showdown build publishes an entry-mapped, legal baseline before research, so the remaining delivery work protects the ENHANCED file (the one carrying strategy), not the existence of a file.
2. **Honest gate separation.** Every check is exactly one of these, and Mixed checks are split into their facts, never disabled wholesale:
   - **V** (submission validity or authority): IDs, salaries, eligibility, slots, entry mapping, template structure, locks, exact delivered bytes. Never faked, never relaxed.
   - **S** (strategic preference): exposure, stacks, overlap, reuse, anti-correlation, ownership. Relaxed autonomously when necessary unless an explicit, current never-relax applies (distinct lineups per contest is one: F-3).
   - **P** (process, model confidence, bookkeeping, research completeness): recorded truthfully, and never alone a reason to withhold an independently V-valid file.

   An S or P failure changes the label to review-grade. It never removes the file. "Upload-ready" keeps CLAUDE.md's meaning: a certified Classic export with all three certifications passing.
3. **One source of truth:** this file, enforced by `LINT`.
4. **Session sizing:** one window, one breakpoint, one objective command. The full gate costs 4m45s in a cloud container (`docs/hosts.md`) and runs once at the start and once at the landing; an M row that touches both `BS` (8,036 lines) and `EP` (7,742 lines) is split at its breakpoint before it starts, because reading both is most of a window.

**Priority rule (Ben, 2026-09-25: rank by what wins large amounts of money; replaces the 2026-09-22 D>V>C>T>P>G>H order).** Every open row was re-ranked on two questions, in this order: (1) has its absence cost a delivered file or put entries into the wrong prize structure (fees lost, a seat forgone)? (2) does it change what the engine builds toward the cohorts the archive says win the contests we enter (fees turned into seats and prizes)? Cost and dependencies then set the sequence inside a tier. The reconciliation with 2026-09-22: delivery items stay at the top only where a file has actually been lost or stranded (`R419`, `R132`, `R404`, the deadline default, the thin bank, F-3 on the Showdown ladder); the defensive tail of the delivery audit (validators, telemetry, acceptance suites) moves behind the construction work. The tiers, in queue order:

- **Tier 1, stop losing fees.** Cheap delivery holes with a lost or stranded file behind them.
- **Tier 2, win more with what we enter.** Construction changes the archive supports, cheapest first; a labelled review proxy grades each after ~12 conditioned slates.
- **Tier 3, the strategic file finishes.** The larger delivery items (relaxation that finishes, partial salvage, the deadline reaching the solvers).
- **Tier 4, input truth that changes lineups.** Who is batting, who is pitching, hands, locks, the market.
- **Tier 5, margin inside the window.** Throughput.
- **Tier 6, measure toward the next construction change.** The mining, grading and modeling lane; steers nothing until graded.
- **Tier 7, guard, record, hygiene.**
- **ARCHIVE** rows, then the **Deferred** pools.

**Walls every row keeps** (CLAUDE.md):
- no scripted DK access;
- never trim the legal pool (a fast solve means MILP time or gap limits, never a player cut);
- `scipy.optimize.milp` only;
- sorted sets and `PYTHONHASHSEED=0`;
- `runs/<id>/final/DKEntries.csv` is never rewritten;
- a file already presented to Ben is never silently superseded;
- no sim ladder, EV core, field-ROI engine or greenfield rebuild while R13 and R10 stand (`docs/backlog.md`, "Do not build").

## Where the money is (observed outcomes from the archive; review proxies, never probabilities)

- **What we enter.** Cheap satellites and micro GPPs: 285 of 422 archived contests are satellites; the 2026-09-24 file was 12 entries at $0.10-$1.00 across seven contests. A satellite pays ONE seat in 104 of 123 mined-window rows, so "cash" and "win" are the same event there and the washout axis is the portfolio (ledger 3.21, R258).
- **Classic.** Top-decile cohorts are chalk-positive against their own field everywhere (satellites +13.7pp cumulative ownership, replicated), carry fewer sub-10% bats, and favour 5-2-1 on 3g+ slates (+13 to +17pp, both halves). Our entries ran chalk-negative (−7.6pp → −21.4pp across the season) and built 5-2-1 at 0.4% against a field at 25.7%. R340 (2026-09-15) made the five-stack floor and quota live from every door; their 12-slate grade starts from there.
- **Showdown.** Pitcher CPT is the strongest replicated construction signal in either format (56.8% of top-decile, 65.5% of winners, field 46%; ours 40%). 5-1 is directional-positive but we over-concentrate (73% vs the top-1% cohort's 50%). Captain chalk flips with field size: under 100 entries, winners captain the chalk (45% owned); at 1k-10k, leverage captains win. Our satellite entries drifted contrarian exactly while the fields got chalkier.
- **Where fees have been lost, or can be.** A file undelivered on 2138_4g (R132); a stranded baseline on every autobuild kill (R419) and a stranded uncertified file on a rung crash (R459); a live repair blocked by a feed demand DK's own column answered (R404); the preflight command late swap prints refusing every legal post-first-pitch swap (R450); F-3 advisory-only in both referees, so a repaired or hand-built file can pay twice into one contest (R444, R426); a night-before `--deliver-by` governing every cap open at once (R460); one IL bat inside a posted Showdown nine dropping the thesis ladder silently (R456); a re-run served the previous build's bank under a control that never took (R453); two of ten Classic slots priced blind to the market (R433).
- **What the prior is worth today.** Ordering usable (median Spearman 0.45-0.64), level compressed; 46.5% of its roster budget lands on players with zero observed ownership, and a pre-registered availability challenger cut complete-pool MAE 40.8% held out (R342): the cheapest large improvement on the board, and it costs no new data.
