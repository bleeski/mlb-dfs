Release_Type: Workflow reliability release addressing the structural causes of the missed-lock incidents that persisted regardless of engine version. (1) Pool contract: live_data_adapters.build_slate_pool is THE required intake step, restricting every build to confirmed-lineup hitters (Batting_Order plus stamped F2 from the posted slot), the platoon-projected nine for each TBD team (slots via platoon_order_by_player_id, never Batting_Order), and probable or explicitly declared starters; every other salary row is dropped before the engine sees it, absent rather than Excluded, ending the full-CSV projection waste and the non-starter pitcher-role confusion. (2) Slate clock: slate_intake_manager.slate_clock detects the first game lock from the salary Game Info column or the feed lock map and computes the T-5 delivery deadline; run_slate surfaces it on every checkpoint and result with warnings when the window is inside 15 minutes or past. The standing delivery rule: the best certified file is presented no later than T-5, refinements via run_late_swap. (3) Percentage exposure caps join the feasibility floors (pitcher, primary-stack, and player pct caps floor to the structural demand of the pitcher and stack rotations), retiring the manual pct-cap relaxation of ledger 3.3, and floors now apply only to keys the posture merge produced so a floor may relax but never add a cap. (4) Workflow cuts: the tail scanner, contested-slot audit, and platoon mispricing screen are opt-in only; the fill-depth plan stays computed but leaves the default report; the three-assumption kill list is replaced by a single Blockers line; the session-start ledger read is replaced by the ledger Quick Card. MILP hard constraints, certification gates, and the provenance chain are untouched; all additions are deterministic review inputs, never ROI, win-rate, or probability claims

Release guarantees:
- Single front door (run_slate) assembles raw slate inputs, derives contest postures, builds the candidate bank, and runs the certified path; approve=False returns the pre-build checkpoint and creates no run.
- STRATEGY_DEFAULTS are labeled principled priors, never win rate or ROI; merged portfolio controls take the tightest cap across contests, are then floored to a slate-feasible minimum, and any relaxation or applied floor is surfaced.
- Coverage-guaranteed candidate bank (v2.25.0): resolve_candidate_bank_size no longer collapses a large field to a hard 40 (10+ scales as ceil(requested*2) under DEFAULT_CANDIDATE_BANK_CAP=150), and build_diverse_candidate_bank forces guaranteed SP-pair and stack coverage into the auto-bank by building legal forced-pair, forced-stack lineups deduped by exact player set until the viable SP pairs are covered and the target size is reached; run_slate builds through it and surfaces candidate_bank["diversity_augmentation"]. It adds candidates, changes no projection and no MILP hard constraint, and is a deterministic review input, never a win-rate, ROI, or probability claim.
- Feasibility-aware control resolution (v2.25.0): _merged_controls_for_build accepts feasibility_floors and raises the tightest-merged max_sp_pair_repetition to ceil(entries/viable_sp_pairs) and max_shared_players to min(9, max_stack + 2*(pair repeats) + 1) after the min-merge and before the explicit override, so a mixed portfolio no longer inherits a pair cap of 1 from a single-entry posture or a shared-players cap of 6 that is infeasible against a repeating five-stack; the explicit override always wins, and applied floors and any override-over-floor are reported under controls_feasibility.
- Pre-solve feasibility assertion (v2.25.0): run_slate injects checkpoint["feasibility"] via _feasibility_report at the approve=False checkpoint; it is structural and bank-free, checks SP-pair capacity (viable_pairs * cap >= entries) and the shared-players floor, and when a check fails it names the exact binding constraint and required cap value in the checkpoint warnings. It is advisory and never a hard block.
- Assembly conformance coerces positions to DK slot tokens, defaults Ownership_Tier to Mid when no ownership feed exists, and defaults Stack_Group to team.
- The light_satellite path skips only the bank-coverage solve and retains the immutable run directory, the final-export re-read, and the blank-reserved-row block.

- Exact Entry ID and ordered ten-slot reconciliation.

- Diagnostics recomputed from and hash-bound to the final DKEntries export.

- Fresh retractable-roof status is mandatory.

- The bank-diversification guardrail is a deterministic review proxy, never a win-rate, ROI, or probability claim: contested_slot_audit surfaces top-ceiling single-position hitters that a comparable, more flexible hitter can crowd out of the bank (reported in the pre-build checkpoint and as a checkpoint warning), audit_bank_player_coverage flags any top-ceiling hitter absent from every candidate on every full build regardless of how the bank was built, and generate_positional_variant_candidates builds forced-lock variants to cover a flagged slot; the guardrail surfaces and never auto-applies an override.
- Export writing refuses pre-existing outputs and never deletes on failure.
- Run status ends as promoted, blocked, or diagnostic, never building.
- Adapters never log API keys and accept pre-fetched JSON without network egress.
- Optional xwOBA baseline correction is a context-neutral, deterministic multiplier applied to the proxy Base before F1-F5, PA-gated and clipped, falling back to AvgPointsPerGame, labeled a review input and never a profitability claim; the Savant CSVs are per-slate inputs and live fetching stays outside the audited engine.
- The xwOBA correction is joined to the slate ONLY by the registered, checksummed DK-keyed build helper (build_dk_keyed_corrections), which re-keys Savant MLBAM ids onto DraftKings draftable ids by name match, keeps the higher-PA row on a name collision and reports it, and surfaces the match rate; run_slate wires this join into _assemble_projection_frame, and the raw MLBAM-keyed map is never applied to the DK-keyed frame. An unmatched or sub-floor player falls back to the uncorrected AvgPointsPerGame. A supplied correction that matches zero players on a pool of at least ten raises as a wiring error rather than silently building on uncorrected Bases; a match rate below 50% and Savant CSVs supplied without AvgPointsPerGame values are surfaced as warnings, never silent.
- The value-sanity guard is engine code, not a manual protocol step: hitter Base is capped at the slate 90th-percentile hitter points-per-$1k times 1.08 before F1-F5; pitchers are never capped; clips are Notes-tagged, reported under projection_enrichment, and warned in the checkpoint; the opt-out (apply_value_sanity_guard=False) is itself reported.
- Per-player Ceiling multipliers from expected ISO are a deterministic prior on right-tail shape, never a probability claim: xISO percentile within the qualified Savant table, PA-shrunk with the shared 50/100 discipline, clipped 1.25-1.60 around the 1.42 neutral, joined by the same name crosswalk as the xwOBA correction, consumed by build_projections through the per-row Ceiling_Multiplier column with unmatched rows at the uniform neutral; this block covers hitters, with pitcher ceilings carried by the K-rate input below; the Ceiling >= Floor hard error still governs.
- Per-pitcher Ceiling multipliers from a K-rate input are a deterministic prior on right-tail shape, never a probability claim: the FanGraphs season pitching CSV is a per-slate data input, never checksummed; K% is preferred over K/9 when both exist; the percentile universe is starter rows only (GS >= 1 when a GS column exists) so reliever K rates cannot depress starter percentiles and a GS-0 arm takes the neutral; sample weight is batters faced (real TBF column when present, else IP x 4.25) shrunk under the shared 50/100 discipline; multiplier = 1.42 + (percentile - 0.5) x 0.30 clipped 1.25-1.60; the DK re-key runs through the same name normalizer as the xwOBA and xISO joins, pitchers only, higher-TBF row wins a name collision and the collision is reported; run_slate(fangraphs_pitching_csv=...) fills the same per-row Ceiling_Multiplier column, Notes-tags applied rows, and surfaces projection_enrichment["pitcher_ceiling"]; a supplied file matching zero pitchers on a pool of at least ten raises as a wiring error; unmatched, non-starter, or sub-floor arms take the uniform neutral so the column stays strictly additive.
- The deterministic F4 is a labeled prior, never a win-rate, ROI, or probability claim: opposing-SP Savant xwOBA-against over the league mean (PA-shrunk, clipped 0.90-1.10) times the F4_PLATOON_PRIOR hand table, combined clip 0.85-1.15; the SP-quality component applies team-wide even without batter hands so TBD lineups still receive it; consumed via run_slate f4_by_player_id and applied only to rows lacking an explicit F4, so a hand-supplied factor always wins; applied rows are Notes-tagged and surfaced; pitcher F4 stays 1.0 in this version, gated on a team-level opposing-lineup input (team K% and wOBA vs hand), not the pitcher's own K rate.
- run_slate surfaces projection_enrichment (xwoba, ceiling, pitcher_ceiling, value_guard, f4, warnings) in both the pre-build checkpoint payload and the approved result, appends enrichment warnings to the checkpoint warnings, and notes on the projections_override path that assembly enrichments do not apply to a prebuilt frame.
- Projection-enrichment wiring coverage: fourteen tests drive synthetic salary, Savant, and FanGraphs fixtures through the real joins and assert non-neutral corrections, ceilings, pitcher ceilings, and F4 values demonstrably reach the frame, both zero-match wiring errors raise, K% outranks K/9, the DK-keyed pitcher join maps pitchers only and resolves name collisions to the higher-TBF row, the K-rate and xISO values coexist in one Ceiling_Multiplier column, and the value guard caps the outlier hitter, never a pitcher, and honors the opt-out.
- Pool contract (v2.26.0): build_slate_pool is the required intake front door; hitters are the confirmed nine per posted lineup plus the platoon-projected nine per TBD team, pitchers are the feed probables plus explicit declarations, and every other salary row is absent from the frame. The salary CSV stays authoritative for the players kept; a TBD team the platoon data cannot fill falls back to top-9 AvgPointsPerGame with a loud warning or is excluded on request; postponed games are excluded; a team with no probable and no declared arm is a surfaced blocker. Output bundles run_slate-ready kwargs, the F4 building blocks, the slate clock, and a pool report with per-team status, warnings, and blockers. Deterministic intake bookkeeping that moves no projection.
- Slate clock (v2.26.0): slate_clock parses ET game datetimes from the salary Game Info column or accepts the feed lock map, names the first lock, and computes the delivery deadline at lock minus 5 minutes; run_slate surfaces checkpoint["slate_clock"] and result["slate_clock"] and warns when the deadline is inside 15 minutes or past. Deterministic bookkeeping, never a guarantee the session meets it.
- Percentage exposure floors (v2.26.0): _slate_feasibility derives the minimum feasible pitcher, primary-stack, and player pct caps from the entry count, viable SP count, and stackable-team count; run_slate floors the tightest-merged caps before the explicit override, which still wins; _feasibility_report carries matching capacity checks naming the exact required cap value; floors apply only to keys the posture merge produced, so a floor may relax but never add a cap.
- Pool, clock, and pct-floor test coverage (v2.26.0): the pool keeps only field players (bench bats and non-probable arms absent), stamps confirmed orders with F2 while platoon slots ride the projected-order map, falls back loudly on missing platoon data, surfaces missing probables as blockers and postponed games as exclusions; the clock parses Game Info ET datetimes and computes deadline math from either source; the pct floors are derived, applied only when the key exists, overridden explicitly, and reach the merged controls end-to-end with the clock and its warning on the checkpoint.
- Coverage and feasibility test coverage (v2.25.0): the diverse candidate bank covers every viable SP pair with legal, non-duplicate lineups and is never worse than the base bank on pair coverage or count; the feasibility floor raises the merged repetition caps and the explicit override wins over the floor; _slate_feasibility derives the correct pair count and floors; and the run_slate checkpoint surfaces feasibility and flags an infeasible explicit override by name without blocking or creating a run.
- The platoon-order adapter (platoon_order_adapter.py) is opt-in and upstream of the certified solve. build_projected_order emits a projected {Player_ID: slot} map crosswalked to DraftKings ids via the salary file using the canonical name normalizer, never sets confirmed_teams, and is superseded by a posted lineup through refresh_confirmed_lineups; run_slate applies it as Batting_Order and F2 = batting_order_factor(slot) on rows lacking an order before build_projections, leaves confirmed or explicit orders untouched, and surfaces the applied set under projected_order. The FanGraphs platoon-lineups JSON is a per-slate data input, not a checksummed engine file.
- The platoon mispricing screen is a deterministic review proxy that compares tonight's projected slot to the player's RHP/LHP-frequency-weighted typical slot and reports the F2-implied lift; it never auto-applies, never changes a projection, and is never a win-rate, ROI, or probability claim.
- The results tracker, calibration engine, slate simulation, and post-slate evaluator are parked in parked/, are below their signal thresholds at current slate volume, and are excluded from the audited engine; their tests live in parked/test_parked.py.
