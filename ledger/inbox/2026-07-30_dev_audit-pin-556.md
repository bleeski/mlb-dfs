## What changed, for the archive record

`min_five_stack_share_pct` is a new portfolio control and the FIRST lower bound
in the joint allocator MILP; every other control there is a ceiling. It ships at
0.0 on `wta_satellite` and is absent from every other posture, so no build
behaves differently until someone raises it through
`portfolio_controls_override`.

Two facts ARCHIVE will want when reading briefs later:

1. Candidates now carry `primary_stack_size` alongside `primary_stack`, and
   allocator assignment rows carry it too. A candidate built before 2026-07-30
   reports 0, which cannot satisfy a size floor. That is deliberate: an unknown
   size must not count toward a floor it may not meet.
2. The merge rule for the new floor is not the ceiling rule. One posture silent
   on the floor retires it for the whole merge, and among postures that declare
   it the LEAST demanding wins. A ceiling merges to the tightest because one
   portfolio must satisfy every contest's ceiling; a floor merges the other way
   because the same portfolio must stay legal for the contest that never asked
   for it.

Motivating evidence is `ledger/2026-07-30_field_shape_analysis.md`, and it is an
observed outcome plus a deterministic descriptive statistic, not a win rate. It
does not license turning the control on by itself.
