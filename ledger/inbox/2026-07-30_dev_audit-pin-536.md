## Worth a Quick Card line in its own right

The per-slate loop changed shape. When Ben pastes lineups, the build no longer
fetches them: `tools/lineups_from_paste.py` converts the paste to the normal
feed and the API feed is fallback for uncovered sides only. A fully pasted slate
reads no platoon reference, so the 7-day staleness blocker has nothing to gate on
those builds. SKILL.md carries the procedure.

One operational fact ARCHIVE will care about when reading old briefs: feeds now
carry `source: operator_paste` or `api_feed_fallback` per SIDE, so provenance of
a confirmed lineup is answerable after the fact. Feeds written before 2026-07-30
carry no source key at all.
